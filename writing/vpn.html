VPN: What is a VPN and how does it work?

CATEGORY: CYBERSECURITY / NETWORKS

[ ↓ RAW .ODT ]

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) is a service that creates an encrypted digital tunnel between your device (phone, laptop, PC) and a remote server managed by the VPN provider.

Your Device → Encrypted Tunnel → VPN Server → Internet

How does it work, step by step?

  • Data encryption: Before your data traffic leaves your device, the VPN transforms it into an indecipherable code.
  • The encrypted tunnel: That traffic travels through your Internet provider (ISP) or the Wi-Fi network you are connected to, but no one on that network can see the content of your data or the exact pages you access.
  • Identity mask (IP): The VPN server receives your encrypted data, decrypts it and sends it to the Internet. To the websites or services you visit, the connection does not come from your home or your phone, but from the VPN server, hiding your real IP address.

Why use one?

Using a VPN is fundamentally useful for most people for three everyday reasons: protecting personal information on high-risk networks, maintaining privacy from those who administer the connection, and enabling completely secure remote access. Beyond serving to bypass regional blocks or censorship, this technology offers very practical solutions both for daily life and the workplace, making it an accessible tool for any user.

In everyday life, the VPN works as an invisible shield every time you connect to the Wi-Fi of a café, a hotel or an airport. By encrypting all the information going in and out of your device, it prevents curious onlookers or cybercriminals connected to the same network from intercepting your passwords, messages or banking data. In the same way, it prevents your Internet provider or the owner of the place where you connect from tracking the exact pages you visit, which reduces the harassment of targeted advertising and makes it harder for phone companies to slow your speed based on what you are viewing. It is also a great ally when traveling, since it keeps your activity shielded from the surveillance of unknown networks in other countries.

In the professional and personal sphere, a VPN is the key to connecting distant locations privately. Many companies and schools use it so that their employees or students can access internal systems, shared folders and work tools from home without risking the organization's security. On a personal level, it allows you to connect your phone or laptop to the devices in your home, such as a hard drive with your photos or personal folders, securely while you are away. It can even help you regain access to websites blocked by strict network filters at your school or workplace, although this should always be done responsibly.

Which ones do we recommend?

When choosing a service, the best alternatives are those that prioritize their users' privacy from the ground up, minimizing the collection of personal data when creating accounts and in payment methods, and avoiding invasive identity verification processes (KYC). Under this anonymity-focused approach, the most recommendable options are Mullvad, Obscura, Nym, IVPN, Xeovo and Cryptostorm.
